19

[ the system ] · P12One idea. One core piece. Then every format.

This is the whole idea of tonight. Never four separate ideas for four networks. One idea becomes one core piece. Then you cut that piece into one version per network. Same message everywhere, and you only think once.

20

[ my workflow ] · P12bEvery idea goes through the same four steps.

Here is how it works for me. I have ideas all day. I post them straight to Claude, in Notion, or every day in one database I call Raw content. From a raw idea, I make one core piece: a LinkedIn post or a YouTube video. Then I cut it into one version per network. It always starts from one single piece of content.

21

[ one real idea · 23 oct ] · P12cIt started with a video I saw: Jesse Itzler’s 1-6-4 rule.

Now one real idea, all the way. 23 October: I see a video of Jesse Itzler on Facebook, his 1-6-4 rule. I talk about it with Claude. That night, a robot reads my day and files the idea in Raw content, ready for a LinkedIn post and the newsletter. Every post I publish lives in one Notion database, by network and by date. Same structure as the template you’ll get.

22

[ core piece · same day ] · P12dSame day: one 39-second motion video.
