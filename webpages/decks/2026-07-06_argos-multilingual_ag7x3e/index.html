Why this approach

07 · Why this approach

Why this, and not the alternatives

vs adding another tool

An 8th tool means an 8th silo. The point is not more software, it is one memory. We consolidate, and the tools that earn their place stay: your internal platform does.

vs building it internally

Your developers already build annotation tooling, and that is exactly where their time pays off. An ops backbone is a different craft: architecture, governance, adoption. Your internal bot already showed what AI on scattered data delivers.

vs waiting

The chaos was absorbable at 4 PMs. At 14, with a GM asking for P&L, every month adds history that will have to be migrated someday. Waiting makes the project bigger, not smaller.
