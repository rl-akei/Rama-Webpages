03 · What I see

The root cause under the surface

The root cause

Your workflow is linear: Draft, Copy, Translation, Design, Approval. That is perfect for one team passing work down a line. It breaks the moment one request needs several teams in parallel, each with its own deadline. The subtask tables you built are a symptom, not the problem.

If nothing changes

Every new market and every bigger campaign adds more hidden tables, more priority calls made by hand, more work depending on one person. The system that scaled you to 200 people becomes the thing that slows the next 100.

04 · The solution

A new architecture, same daily habits

We rebuild the logic underneath, not the surface your 200 users already know.

account_tree

True subtasks + linked tickets

Group a project, split the work across teams, and keep every piece visible on the right board.

dashboard

Personal dashboards

One page per person with everything assigned to them, subtasks included. No more hunting across views.

bolt

Priority automation · add-on

A daily agent that ranks every ticket by department, then deadline. Runs on its own usage cost.

fact_check

Guided ticket creation

A mandatory form so no ticket ever lands half-filled, and assignments go to groups, not one name.
